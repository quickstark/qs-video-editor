# HyperFrames Visual Identity: Documentary Wedding

## Style prompt

Create a warm, restrained documentary-wedding graphic system that feels editorial and human rather than templated. Use generous ivory negative space, near-charcoal type, a single muted-wine accent, hairline rules, and deliberate typography. Motion should be quiet, deterministic, and subordinate to real footage and speech.

## Colors

- Background: #F6F1EA
- Rule: #D6CBC0
- Accent: #7B4D4F
- Accent soft: #D9C0B8
- Ink: #28231F
- Muted: #6F6761

## Typography

- Display: Cormorant Garamond Semibold
- Supporting and captions: Inter Regular/Medium
- Temporary preview fallbacks: Georgia and Arial
- Final renders require verified font files or a documented supported-font substitution.

## Motion

- Build the fully visible hero layout before animating.
- Entrances: 0.45-0.9 seconds; stagger related elements by 0.12-0.25 seconds.
- Multi-scene graphic transitions: restrained crossfade or clean reveal.
- Use deterministic, seekable timelines only.
- Hold titles long enough for a comfortable read; clear them before an emotionally central vow line.

## What not to do

- No calligraphy fonts, faux luxury, excessive gold, generic wedding slogans, or template flourishes.
- No glitch, whip-pan, film-burn, fake light leak, emoji, or wall-clock/random animation.
- No generated faces, people, venues, weather, objects, or events presented as wedding evidence.
- No word-pop vow captions, bouncing picture-in-picture, or animated decoration competing with speech.
- No unverified date, location, title, identity, or quotation.

The canonical detail lives in brand.md and styles/documentary-wedding/style.md. If they conflict, stop and request a style decision.
